Job Automation

Job Automation is a personal, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It keeps track of job advertisements, drafts application documents from the owner's own records, and keeps private backups of those documents.

When the owner connects it to Google, it uses the narrow drive.file permission only: it can see and manage solely the files it creates itself, inside the owner's own Google Drive. It cannot read anything else in that Drive, and the data never leaves the owner's own computer and Drive.

It has no other users, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and it shares nothing with anyone.
