Job Automation — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What this app is

Job Automation is a personal, single-user application used by its owner on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What it stores

It stores job advertisements it has read, the owner's own career records, and the application documents it drafts from them — all on the owner's own computer. It runs no server, and no copy of this data is sent to anyone.

Google user data

When the owner connects the app to Google, it requests the narrowest available permission, drive.file ("see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). With it, the app creates and maintains a private backup folder in the owner's own Google Drive, containing only files the app itself created: the owner's own generated documents and record archives.

The app cannot see or touch any other file in the owner's Drive. It does not read, collect or process any other Google user data. It does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell or share it, does not use it to train artificial-intelligence models, and does not allow anyone to read it.

Who can see it

Only the owner. There is no third party, no analytics provider and no advertising network involved. Data is used solely to provide the app's own features: drafting the owner's application documents and backing them up to the owner's own Drive.

Retention, deletion and revocation

The backup files live in the owner's own Google Drive and can be deleted there at any time; deleting them removes them for good. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Third-party apps & services. Revoking access stops all further use immediately.
